# Vortex — what's new compared to the original

## 1. Built for phones: column layout
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/01-phone-column.png)
A full touch combat HUD on a 20:9 phone. Weapons and drones sit in a column next to a large TARGET panel, the subsystems move to the bottom row, and every control is big enough to tap.

## 2. Pan layout
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/02-pan-phone.png)
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/02-pan-fold.png)
An optional layout, on phones and on the open Fold, puts both ships in one wide scene that you pan with the edge arrows or a two-finger drag, with the full HUD still on screen.

## 3. Galaxy Z Fold, open: split layout
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/03-fold-split.png)
On the almost-square inner screen, your ship stands upright on one side and the enemy fills the other, with the HUD placed around both.

## 4. HUD calibration
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/04-hud-calibration.png)
You can scale the top bar, the bottom bar and the weapons and drones column separately, or leave them on AUTO. The settings are saved for each form factor (phone, Fold cover, Fold inner).

## 5. Touch power toggle
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/05-power-toggle.png)
The +/− button switches what a tap does: in + mode tapping a system, weapon or drone adds power; in − mode it removes it. Holding one finger and tapping with another also removes power, so no long press is ever needed.

## 6. Built-in quality-of-life mod
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/06-qol-mod-options.png)
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/06-qol-in-combat.png)
An optional mod, built only on the public mod hooks and off by default, can be switched on one group at a time from MOD OPTIONS: weapon cooldown timers in seconds, hull numbers on both ships, colour-coded health bars, a game-speed control (− 1x +) and more.

## 7. Sharp at any resolution
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/07-pc-2k.png)
The game renders at the display's full resolution, not as a scaled-up 1280×720 image, so text and HUD lines stay sharp at 1440p and above.

## 8. Multiple profiles
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/08-profiles.png)
There are three separate save profiles. Each shows its unlock and achievement progress, and each can be selected, unlocked or deleted from the main menu.

## 9. Autobattle live wallpaper
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/09-autobattle-ultrawide.png)
![](https://github.com/workarcho/vortex/releases/download/showcase/09-wallpaper-button.png)
An AI-vs-AI autobattle mode fills any aspect ratio, including 21:9. On PC, the main menu exports it as an HTML wallpaper for Wallpaper Engine, built from your own game data.
